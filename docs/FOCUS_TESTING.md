# Camera focus test

## Run locally

Run `npm run dev`, open the localhost address, and log in. Use a recent desktop Chrome or Edge with a webcam. `predev` and `prebuild` copy the pinned MediaPipe runtime and download the version-1 Face Landmarker model on first setup. The model's SHA-256 is checked. Assets are then served by this app; no camera frames or landmarks are uploaded. Camera access requires localhost or HTTPS.

## Expected behavior

- The camera starts **off**. Enable camera previews tracking without recording time. Turning the camera off or stopping a session releases the webcam and worker.
- When no focus session is running, open an unfinished task. Its popup shows optional focus checkboxes beside levels, if present, then a **Focus note (optional)** text box and a **Focus** button underneath. Start focus with no levels selected and an empty note, or choose levels and/or enter a note. Focus checkboxes do not mark levels complete. Existing level-completion controls still update progress.
- Alternatively choose **Focus** below the camera and select one or more unfinished tasks. Each selected task offers optional levels and a note; large level lists are paginated. Multi-task selections are one shared session, not duplicate time per task. Selected level names and notes are saved with the session and appear in active-session details, welcome-back details, calendar history, and JSON export. History uses account cloud sync with a local browser backup and retry queue.
- Eye/iris markers are drawn over the mirrored preview. The presence status is an experimental visibility estimate, not proof of productive work, identity, or gaze at a particular screen location.
- Eyes must remain visible for one second to start/resume focus. Missing eyes must persist for ten seconds to start an absence; the absence is backdated to the first missing-eye observation. Blinks and eye loss under ten seconds are ignored when camera observations continue. Poor light, covered eyes, and head turns can look like absence.
- An absence of **up to and including five minutes** is a **Short break**. More than five minutes is a **Long break**. The application does not infer that a break was a washroom visit.
- Stable eyes after an absence automatically resume timing and show a welcome-back dialog listing the selected tasks. An existing task/editor dialog is not interrupted; the welcome-back dialog waits until it closes.
- Pause is explicit and cannot automatically resume from eye detection. Select Resume to wait for eyes again. Stop ends the session and turns off the camera. Returning does not complete tasks or award XP.
- Camera failures, multiple faces, and stale observations are untracked, not focus or absence. The frame watchdog allows at most five seconds after the last observation before marking a gap. Old results are rejected. Closing the browser, sleeping, or browser/OS suspension cannot record focus.
- On browsers exposing `MediaStreamTrackProcessor` on the window, the app transfers a camera-frame stream to its worker. **Direct camera stream · background enabled** confirms this capture mode. It does not depend on preview rendering or dashboard capture timers, so fresh eye observations can continue while working in another app. GPU inference is preferred, with CPU initialization fallback. Capture uses 320 × 240 frames and analyzes up to five frames per second. A temporary clone of the same camera track is stopped on fallback or camera shutdown.
- Other browsers use **Preview capture fallback · tiny monitor recommended**. This mode can be throttled when hidden. After starting focus, select **Tiny monitor ↗** in a supported desktop Chrome/Edge browser. It requests a 240 × 170 px always-on-top window; browser minimum sizes and the native title bar may make it larger. It has Pause/Resume, Stop, and Dock controls. Closing or docking restores the preview to the dashboard. Visibility alone never changes the time category; fresh observations determine focus/away, and actual gaps become untracked.
- The camera diagnostics show processing duration and delivery delay for each result. If results take more than five seconds, they are rejected rather than counted as current focus. Background camera delivery still depends on the browser and operating system; it is not a guarantee against suspension.
- The calendar shows focus and away totals for each day in the saved account time zone. Week and Day compare planned blocks with recorded segments; Month retains daily summaries. Select an Actual block or daily summary for task names, break episodes, paused/untracked totals, and JSON export. Sessions crossing midnight are split across the correct dates. Start Focus from a plan to prefill its task, selected levels, and note; the recording retains a planned-occurrence reference.

## Storage

Time records sync to the signed-in Supabase account after applying `supabase/migrations/202611020001_calendar_focus.sql`; see [CALENDAR_SETUP.md](CALENDAR_SETUP.md). The browser retains `lifeos.focus.v1:<account ID>` as a cache and a durable account-specific upload journal. Existing browser history is imported idempotently, and its original JSON is backed up under `lifeos.focus.backup.v1:<account ID>`. Failed uploads remain queued for reconnection/refocus/Retry sync. Clearing browser data removes unsent records; confirmed cloud history remains available on another browser. Only one tab per account in a browser can record at a time (Web Locks). Checkpoints save/sync every five seconds and on state changes/Stop; a crash may lose the last few seconds. Finalized sessions cannot be overwritten by stale checkpoints. Reopening never counts closed-browser time or automatically turns the camera on. Interrupted checkpoints are labelled. Export history if storage reports a save error.

## Black-box checklist

1. Open the dashboard: camera off, no permission request, and no time recorded.
2. Enable camera: accept the browser prompt yourself. Confirm that the live image is mirrored and markers follow your eyes. Disable it and confirm the browser's camera indicator stops.
3. Open a task with levels. Start with an empty note and no focus checkboxes selected; confirm focus starts after stable eyes. Stop, reopen, choose levels across multiple pages and enter a note, then start again. Confirm selected level names and note appear, completion/XP do not change from the focus selections, and calendar history/export retain the details after stopping/reloading. Repeat for a task without levels and for the camera's multi-task Focus chooser. Blink normally; no break should appear.
4. Cover the webcam for nine seconds, then return: no break should appear. Cover it or leave the frame for at least 12 seconds: confirm focus stops, away time increases, and the label is Short break. The absence includes the initial ten-second confirmation period.
5. Return for at least one second. Confirm focus resumes and the welcome-back dialog lists the task.
6. Repeat an absence lasting over five minutes. Confirm the label changes to Long break and the calendar preserves the entire absence duration.
7. Stop, then use Focus to select two tasks. Confirm one shared timer, both names, and no doubling of calendar totals.
8. Pause and remain visible: focus time must stay unchanged. Resume explicitly and check timing restarts after stable eyes.
9. Check the capture-mode diagnostic. In **Direct camera stream** mode, leave the tiny monitor closed and switch to another tab/app for two minutes with eyes visible. Return and verify focus increased for that interval, not untracked. Repeat while away from the desk and verify Away increases; return and verify focus resumes. In fallback mode, repeat with Tiny monitor open; closing it must not fabricate focus if camera frames stop. Check Pause/Resume/Stop from the small window. Reload or log out and confirm that the floating window and webcam close. The model must recover without starting a second session. Lock/sleep the computer separately and confirm gaps become untracked rather than assumed focus.
10. Deny permission, disconnect the webcam, or block worker/model loading: confirm an error appears, timing becomes untracked, and Stop/Retry remain usable.
11. Cancel task selection while the camera permission/model load is pending. Granting permission later must not start a cancelled session.
12. Open a second tab with the same account. It must not start another recording while the first tab owns a session. Stop the first and retry in the second.
13. Stop and reload: history remains in the calendar and the camera stays off. After sync, sign in with the same account on another browser and verify that history appears. Log in as another account: its history must be separate. Test a disconnected network while focus is active, Stop, reconnect/reload, and verify queued records sync once with the note/levels retained.
14. Check the calendar on desktop and mobile-width screens. Open details, dismiss with Escape/backdrop, and export JSON. Existing task editing, level completion, and grammar suggestions must still work.

Automated timing and worker checks: `node --experimental-strip-types --test --test-isolation=none tests/focus.test.mjs tests/focus-worker.test.mjs tests/task-progress.test.mjs tests/accounts-model.test.mjs`. Worker checks mock the camera/model and verify stream cleanup, fallback, and GPU-to-CPU initialization; a real webcam/browser test is still required.
